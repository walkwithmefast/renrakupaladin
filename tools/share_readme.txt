Renraku Paladin - offline Shadowrun 5th Edition character builder
==================================================================

How to run it
--------------
Double-click "Renraku Paladin.exe". Nothing to install, no account, no internet needed.
(Windows may warn that it's from an unknown publisher the first time - choose "More info" ->
"Run anyway". It's just not code-signed.) It takes a few seconds to start.

Tip: put this folder somewhere permanent first (Documents, say) rather than running it
from inside the zip or your Downloads, because your characters are saved next to the .exe.

Saving your characters
------------------------
- Each character is saved as its own file in a "characters" folder next to the .exe,
  named after the character (e.g. "Kestrel.rp.json"). Back those files up, or send them
  to someone else - the Import option reads them.
- Press Save (or Ctrl+S) to save. It also saves automatically whenever you switch between
  Build and Play, move to another page, switch characters, or close the window.
- The "Character" menu (top right) has New, Duplicate, Import, Export, "Fill PDF character
  sheet", "Open characters folder" and Delete (deleted characters go to the Recycle Bin).
- Import also reads Chummer5a saves (.chum5).

What's included
-----------------
- Full character creation and play: priorities, attributes, skills, qualities, gear, magic,
  the Matrix, vehicles/drones, and more.
- The complete Shadowrun 5E game data (items, costs, spells, qualities, etc.), several
  supplement books included.
- A fillable character-sheet PDF you can export any character onto.

Rulebook descriptions (from your own PDFs)
--------------------------------------------
Item pages can show a short "From the rulebook" description and a link to the exact page.
That text comes from purchased books, so it isn't included - but if you own the Shadowrun 5E
PDFs, the app can read it from YOUR copies:
  1. Open Settings -> "Rulebook PDFs" and click "Browse..." to pick the folder with your PDFs.
  2. Click "Read descriptions from my PDFs". It takes a few minutes for a full collection
     (about a minute for the core rulebook alone) and only needs doing once - again if you
     add books. Everything stays on your computer.
  3. Book page links now open your PDFs at the right page, and item pages show the text.
It recognizes the books by their content, so your file names don't matter. Anything that
isn't a Shadowrun 5E rulebook is listed as "not recognized" and skipped.

Every description (and every item without one) has an "Edit" button: write your own version,
house rules, notes... Your edits replace the book text for that item on every character, and
"Revert to the rulebook text" undoes them. They're saved in the "rulebook" folder next to
the .exe, alongside "characters".

Questions or something looks wrong? Ask whoever sent you this.
